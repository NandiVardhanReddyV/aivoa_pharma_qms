COMPLAINT DESCRIPTION & OBSERVATION:
During routine morning dispensing in the Outpatient Pharmacy, senior staff observed physical defects across multiple blister strips of Batch PCT-2024-8841. Specifically:
1. Approximately 40% of inspected tablets exhibited horizontal split / capping (the crown of the tablet separated from the body inside the blister cavity).
2. Loose powder debris was present within intact blister pockets.
3. Patients reported difficulty swallowing broken fragments.

RETAIN SAMPLES & STORAGE:
- Physical samples are retained and available for QC return shipment.
- Stored at controlled room temperature (20-25°C) in accordance with label instructions.

REQUESTED ACTION:
Immediate investigation into tableting compression parameters, lot quarantine guidance, and replacement stock dispatch.
